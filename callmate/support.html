About CallMate

FAQ

The call doesn't appear / nothing happens?

Add and select a caller first, then tap Start simulated call. CallMate 1.0.1 uses its own incoming card, clearly labeled as a simulation, rather than the system Phone interface. Answer opens the simulated session timer; Finish simulation returns to Home.

A scheduled call didn't ring on time?

Keep CallMate open to show the incoming card automatically at the end of a countdown. In the background or while locked, a local notification reminds you instead; tap it to open CallMate and start the simulation. Allow notifications, and check Focus settings if a reminder is silent or delayed.

Why is the simulated call silent?

The original cue plays only while the incoming card is visible in the foreground. It respects silent mode and system volume. It stops when you answer, decline, the ring duration ends, or you background the app. CallMate does not use the microphone, CallKit, VoIP, or a persistent background audio service.

How do I switch language / appearance?

In Settings, choose System, Light, or Dark appearance. Languages include English, French, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese, with an option to follow your system language.

Is my data safe?

All data stays on your device and is never uploaded or collected. See our Privacy Policy for details.

常见问题

来电不显示 / 没有反应？

请先添加并选择来电方，再点按「开始模拟来电」。CallMate 1.0.1 使用自己的原创来电卡片，并明确标注模拟用途。接听后可查看模拟会话计时，结束后返回首页。

定时来电到点没响？

保持 CallMate 在前台，倒计时结束时会自动显示来电卡片。App 在后台或设备锁屏时改为本地通知提醒，点按通知打开 App 后开始模拟。请允许通知；专注模式和系统通知设置可能使提醒静音或延迟。

为什么模拟来电没有声音？

原创提示音仅在来电卡片处于前台时播放，尊重静音模式和系统音量。接听、拒接、等待超时或进入后台时会停止。CallMate 不使用麦克风、CallKit、VoIP 或持续后台音频服务。

如何切换语言 / 深浅色？

在设置中可选择跟随系统、浅色或深色主题。支持英语、法语、日语、韩语、简体中文、繁体中文，也可跟随系统语言。

我的数据安全吗？

所有数据仅保存在你的设备本地，不上传、不收集。详见 隐私政策。
